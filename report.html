2. D1 — Educational Problem, Learning Outcomes & Context of Use

Educational Problem: Middle-school students studying Acids, Bases, and Indicators (Class 7–10) frequently attempt to memorise 12 disconnected indicator colour changes by rote without connecting them to chemical behaviour or everyday household substances. For a repeat learner who has already failed the topic once (L5), forgetting a single colour rule triggers test anxiety, cognitive overload, and avoidance. Standard online quizzes worsen this anxiety by marking responses "Wrong" without diagnosing which alternate conception caused the error or adjusting the difficulty of the next task. AcidBase Coach solves this through a calm, resumable 10-minute adaptive tutoring sequence (C4) that pairs an interactive indicator dropper tool with a multi-format adaptive tutor (P4) that diagnoses specific misconceptions and routes the learner to prerequisite lab exploration, visual analogies, or harder challenges.

Three Measurable Learning Outcomes (Bloom's Taxonomy)

  1. LO1 — Classify (Understand): Given indicator observations from Litmus, Turmeric, China Rose, or Phenolphthalein, the learner will accurately classify everyday and laboratory liquids as acidic, basic, or neutral.
  2. LO2 — Predict (Apply): Given an everyday test solution (lemon juice, vinegar, soap water, baking soda, milk of magnesia) and two chosen indicators, the learner will predict the exact resulting colour changes before testing.
  3. LO3 — Explain & Diagnose (Analyse / Evaluate): Given a real-world acid-base scenario (treating an acidic ant sting or washing a turmeric curry stain with soap and lemon), the learner will explain the chemical mechanism in 1–2 sentences and diagnose why common misconceptions fail.

Context of Use: Used at home in the evening during focused 10-minute study blocks (C4) before a school Science re-test on a personal or shared Android phone (360 px mobile-first viewport) or laptop browser, with automatic state persistence in localStorage["et617_24b3925_state"].

  • Answer to the P4 Design Challenge (Explicit 3-Branch Decision Rule in decideAdaptiveOutcome()):
    1. Outcome 1 — HARDER_QUESTION: Triggered when isCorrect === true && attempts === 1 && hintsUsed === 0. Increases difficulty (1 → 2 → 3) and advances Bloom question format (Multiple Choice → Colour Predictor → Open Explanation).
    2. Outcome 2 — ALTERNATIVE_EXPLANATION: Triggered when (attempts === 1 && !isCorrect) || misconceptionTag in ["M1","M2"] || (isCorrect && hintsUsed >= 1). Holds difficulty, displays a visual everyday analogy card (Curry Stain / Acid-Base Seesaw) + misconception contrast, and generates a parallel practice question.
    3. Outcome 3 — PREREQUISITE_INSTRUCTION: Triggered when (attempts >= 2 && !isCorrect) || lowConfidenceRequested === true. Steps back to a guided hands-on Prerequisite Indicator Mini-Lab before serving a foundational stepping-stone question.
